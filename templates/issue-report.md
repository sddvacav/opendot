# OpenDot Engineering issue report template

Draft template. Submit only to a verified project destination. Security and privacy details belong in the private reporting process described in [Security](../SECURITY.md).

## Problem

What were you trying to achieve? What did you expect? What happened instead?

## Public version and environment

List the public release version, relevant adapter/dependency versions, operating-system category, and whether this was an installed release or source checkout. Omit usernames, private machine identifiers, credentials, and private paths.

## Minimal reproduction

Provide a public or synthetic input and the smallest steps needed. State whether a model or external service is involved, and whether reproducing the issue incurs cost or transmits data. Do not include secrets or full environment dumps.

## Evidence

Include a short reviewed error excerpt, relevant output, and check results. Mark which checks passed, failed, were skipped, or were not run. Do not attach private traces or unreviewed archives.

## Impact and workaround

Explain who is blocked and any safe workaround already tried. Do not estimate affected users or severity without a basis.
